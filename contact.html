Why Personal Consultation Matters: The Wilder Spaces Difference

At Wilder Spaces, we believe that every landscape tells a unique story—and that story deserves to be heard before any work begins. That's why our contact process is designed not just to collect information, but to initiate a meaningful dialogue about your property's potential. When you reach out to us, you're not just booking a service; you're beginning a partnership with a team of dedicated horticultural scientists, landscape architects, and ecological stewards who are genuinely invested in the health and beauty of your outdoor environment.

Our consultation process begins with a deep listening session. We want to understand your vision, your property's history, your challenges, and your aspirations. Whether you're dealing with persistent brown patches, want to reduce water consumption, or dream of a complete landscape transformation, our team uses your input to craft a customized approach that aligns with your goals and budget. We don't believe in one-size-fits-all solutions—every property we manage receives a bespoke treatment plan that respects its unique ecological context.

What to Expect When You Contact Us

We've streamlined our communication channels to make it as easy as possible for you to connect with us. Here's what happens when you reach out:

  • Step 1: Initial Inquiry — Whether you call, email, or submit our contact form, your inquiry is routed directly to our Concierge Team. We aim to acknowledge all inquiries within 2 hours during business hours.
  • Step 2: Discovery Call — A dedicated client relations specialist will schedule a brief phone or video call to learn more about your property, your needs, and your timeline. This call typically lasts 15–20 minutes.
  • Step 3: Property Assessment — If appropriate, we'll schedule an on-site consultation where one of our landscape architects will visit your property to conduct a thorough evaluation, including soil sampling, sun exposure analysis, and existing vegetation assessment.
  • Step 4: Custom Proposal — Based on our findings and your input, we'll prepare a detailed, transparent proposal that outlines our recommended services, timeline, and investment. We never pressure you to make a decision—we want you to feel fully informed and confident.
  • Step 5: Welcome to the Wilder Spaces Family — Once you approve the proposal, we'll schedule your first service visit and introduce you to your dedicated account manager. From that point forward, you'll have a direct line to our team for any questions or adjustments.

Frequently Asked Questions About Our Contact Process

We understand that reaching out to a new service provider can come with questions. Here are some of the most common queries we receive:

  • How quickly do you respond to inquiries? We aim to respond to all contact form submissions, emails, and phone calls within 2 business hours during our regular office hours. For urgent matters, please call our main number and follow the prompts for emergency support.
  • Do you offer free consultations? Yes! We provide complimentary, no-obligation initial consultations for all new clients. During this visit, we'll assess your property and provide an overview of our recommended approach.
  • What information should I have ready before contacting you? It's helpful to have your property address, any known details about your current landscaping (e.g., irrigation type, recent issues), and a general idea of your goals. However, even if you don't have all the details, we're happy to guide you through the process.
  • Can I schedule a service immediately? In most cases, we can schedule services within 1–2 weeks of your initial consultation, depending on seasonal availability and the scope of work. We prioritize existing clients for recurring services and work to accommodate new clients as quickly as possible.
  • Is there a minimum service commitment? No. We offer flexible service arrangements, from one-time assessments and projects to comprehensive annual maintenance plans. We tailor our services to fit your specific needs.

Sustainable Landscaping: The Future Starts with a Conversation

As environmental concerns become increasingly urgent, the way we approach landscaping is evolving. At Wilder Spaces, we are committed to sustainable practices that conserve resources, protect local ecosystems, and create resilient landscapes that can adapt to changing climate conditions. When you contact us, you're not just getting a lawn mowing service—you're gaining access to a wealth of knowledge about ecological turf management, water conservation, native plant integration, and organic soil rehabilitation.

We regularly host workshops and webinars on topics like drought-resistant landscaping, organic fertilization techniques, and the importance of biodiversity in residential and commercial settings. By staying in touch with our community through our newsletter and social media channels, you'll be among the first to know about these educational opportunities. We believe that an informed client is a empowered client, and we're dedicated to sharing our expertise to help you make the best decisions for your property and the planet.

Reach out today to start a conversation—not just about your lawn, but about how we can work together to create outdoor spaces that are beautiful, functional, and environmentally responsible. We look forward to hearing from you.
