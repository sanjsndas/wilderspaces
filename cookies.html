Cookie Policy

Understanding how we use cookies to enhance your experience and protect your privacy is essential. This policy explains everything you need to know.

1. Introduction: What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, tablet, smartphone) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, enhance user experience, and provide valuable information to website operators. Cookies are not harmful—they cannot carry viruses or malware, and they cannot access your hard drive.

At Wilder Spaces, we use cookies and similar tracking technologies (such as web beacons, pixels, and local storage) to understand how visitors interact with our website, remember your preferences, and deliver a personalized experience. This Cookie Policy explains what types of cookies we use, why we use them, and how you can control your preferences.

By using our website, you consent to our use of cookies in accordance with this policy, except where you have configured your browser to refuse cookies or where you have explicitly declined cookies through our consent mechanism. We are committed to transparency and giving you control over your data.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

We categorize cookies based on their purpose, duration, and the party that sets them. Below is a detailed breakdown of the cookies we deploy across our digital platforms:

2.1 Strictly Necessary Cookies (Essential)

These cookies are fundamental to the operation of our website. They enable core functionalities such as secure login, session management, and navigation. Without these cookies, our website would not function properly, and you would not be able to use essential features. These cookies do not collect personal data for marketing purposes and are typically set in response to your actions, such as setting privacy preferences, logging in, or filling out forms.

  • Examples: Session ID cookies, CSRF protection tokens, load-balancing cookies.
  • Duration: Usually session-based (deleted when you close your browser) or short-term (a few hours).
  • Opt-out: Not possible while using our website, as they are essential for basic functionality.

2.2 Performance and Analytics Cookies

These cookies allow us to measure and improve the performance of our website by collecting aggregated information about how visitors interact with our content. We use tools like Google Analytics to track metrics such as page views, bounce rates, session duration, and user flows. All data is anonymized and aggregated—we do not use these cookies to identify you individually.

  • Examples: _ga, _gid, _gat (Google Analytics cookies).
  • Duration: Persistent (up to 2 years for _ga) with periodic updates.
  • Opt-out: You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on or by adjusting your cookie preferences.

2.3 Functional and Preference Cookies

These cookies remember your preferences and choices to enhance your browsing experience. For example, they can remember your language preference, region, or font size, so you don't have to reset them on each visit. They also enable features like remembering your login status for a seamless experience.

  • Examples: Language selection cookies, region-specific content cookies.
  • Duration: Persistent (typically 3-12 months).
  • Opt-out: You can disable these cookies, but some features may not work as smoothly.

2.4 Targeting and Advertising Cookies

We use these cookies to deliver relevant advertisements and promotional content that align with your interests. They may be set by us or by third-party advertising partners (e.g., Google Ads, Meta). These cookies track your browsing behavior across different websites and build a profile of your interests. We use them only with your explicit consent.

  • Examples: Google AdSense cookies, remarketing pixels.
  • Duration: Persistent (typically up to 90 days or longer).
  • Opt-out: You can decline these cookies or manage your ad preferences through platforms like Google Ad Settings.

2.5 Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies on our website are set by third-party services that we integrate to enhance our offerings. These include social media plugins (e.g., LinkedIn, Instagram), video embedding (e.g., YouTube), and mapping services (e.g., Google Maps). These third parties may set their own cookies to track your interactions with their content. We do not control these cookies, and we encourage you to review the privacy policies of those third parties.

4. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • Authentication and Security: To verify your identity, manage your session, and protect our website from fraudulent activity.
  • Performance Optimization: To analyze site traffic, identify bottlenecks, and improve page load times.
  • User Experience Personalization: To remember your preferences, such as language and region, to provide a tailored interface.
  • Marketing and Remarketing: To display relevant advertisements based on your interests and track the effectiveness of our marketing campaigns.
  • Social Media Integration: To enable sharing of content on social platforms and to track engagement with our social media presence.
  • Legal Compliance: To record your consent for cookie use as required by data protection regulations (e.g., GDPR, ePrivacy Directive).

5. Your Control Over Cookies

We respect your right to choose whether to accept or decline cookies. You have several options to manage your cookie preferences:

5.1 Cookie Consent Dialogue Box

When you first visit our website, you will see a cookie consent dialogue box at the bottom of the screen. This allows you to accept all cookies, decline non-essential cookies, or customize your preferences. You can change your preferences at any time by clicking the "Cookie Settings" link in the footer of our website.

5.2 Browser Settings

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can choose to block all cookies, delete existing cookies, or set your browser to notify you before a cookie is stored. Please note that blocking essential cookies may impair the functionality of our website.

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and other site data.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data.
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies and site data.

5.3 Third-Party Opt-Out Tools

For advertising cookies, you can opt out of interest-based advertising through industry opt-out platforms such as:

  • YourAdChoices (NAI): optout.networkadvertising.org
  • Digital Advertising Alliance (DAA): optout.aboutads.info
  • Google Ad Settings: adssettings.google.com

Your Privacy Matters: At Wilder Spaces, we believe that you should have full control over your digital footprint. Our cookie consent mechanism is designed to be clear, accessible, and respectful of your choices. You can change your preferences at any time.

6. How Long Do Cookies Stay on My Device?

Cookies have different lifespans depending on their type:

  • Session Cookies: These are temporary and are deleted from your device as soon as you close your browser. They are used for essential functionalities like session management.
  • Persistent Cookies: These remain on your device for a predetermined period, ranging from a few days to several years. They are used to remember your preferences and for analytics purposes. The exact duration for each cookie is listed in the cookie table above.
  • Third-Party Cookies: The lifespan of third-party cookies is determined by the third-party provider. We recommend reviewing their policies for specific details.

You can manually delete cookies at any time through your browser settings. This will remove all cookies stored on your device, and you may need to reset your preferences on our website.

7. Cookies and Personal Data

We do not use cookies to collect personal data that can directly identify you, such as your name, email address, or phone number. However, some cookies (like analytics cookies) collect IP addresses, which may be considered personal data under certain regulations. We treat all data collected through cookies in accordance with our Privacy Policy and applicable laws.

We do not combine the data collected via cookies with other personal data we hold about you unless you have given your explicit consent to do so. For instance, if you complete a contact form, we may link your submitted information with the browsing data to better understand your interests and tailor our communication.
